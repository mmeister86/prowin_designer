## Scope decisions

- TASK-12 is deferred by the user. TASK-42 owns Rybbit integration; TASK-13 removes client captures rather than introducing an inactive tracking abstraction.
- Removing PostHog also removes its Conversations support widget and iframe recorder. Existing help/docs links and frame editing stay available.
- Server callers in `server/mcp.ts` retain their analytics interface, with no network, environment or throttling state in its implementation.

## Review focus

- Legacy keys must not reactivate analytics or outbound relay traffic.
- Removing analytics callbacks must preserve UI actions, promise error handlers and auth/impersonation behavior.
- Frame sandboxing, rendering and inspection must still work without the replay fixture or SDK.
